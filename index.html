Module 1 • Digital Learning Basics

Designing an Effective Learning Journey

This fictional lesson demonstrates how complex material can be transformed into clear, manageable learning content.

Learning objective

By the end of this lesson, the learner should be able to identify three qualities of an effective digital lesson.

Key idea

A strong digital lesson is organised, interactive and accessible. It breaks information into meaningful sections, gives learners opportunities to practise, and works across different devices.

  • Organised: Clear headings and logical sequencing help learners find information quickly.
  • Interactive: Questions, activities and feedback turn passive reading into active learning.
  • Accessible: Good contrast, keyboard support and read-aloud options help more people participate.
Practice point: Technology should make information easier to use—not simply reproduce a long document on a screen.
